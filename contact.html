Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a seat?

Log in with your student account, open the seat map, pick a free seat and choose a time slot.

What happens if I do not arrive on time?

The seat is released automatically when your booking time ends, so other students can use it.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Open your booking history and cancel the reservation before the slot starts.

Who can use this system?

Students can book seats and resources. Library staff use the admin dashboard to manage them.
