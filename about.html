About Us

Learn why we built the Library Seat & Resource Booking System and who is behind it.

Why This Project Exists

In many college libraries, students arrive and find every seat taken, or they leave a bag on a seat and walk away for hours. This wastes space and makes the library crowded.

Our system fixes this. Students can see which seats are free, book a seat for a time slot, and the seat is released automatically when the booking ends. The result is less crowding, fair use of seats, and a simple way to reserve.

Our Objectives

Better Use of Resources

Make sure seats and reading rooms are used well and not left empty.

Less Crowding

Spread students across time slots so the library stays comfortable.

Clear and Fair

Every student can see availability and book in the same simple way.

How It Works

  1. Log in with your student account.
  2. Open the seat map and pick a free seat.
  3. Choose a time slot and confirm your booking.
  4. Use your seat. It is released automatically when the time ends.
