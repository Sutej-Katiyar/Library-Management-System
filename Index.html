Reserve your seat, reading room or library resource in a few clicks.

Book a Seat

About This Project

The Library Seat & Resource Booking System is a web-based platform that helps students reserve library seats, reading rooms and other available resources. A visual seat map shows which seats are occupied and which are free, so students can pick a suitable seat and time slot.

Bookings are recorded, and a seat is released automatically when the booking time ends. This improves how library resources are used, reduces crowding, and gives students a clear and easy way to reserve.

Key Features

Student Login

Log in securely to book and manage your reservations.

Visual Seat Map

See available and occupied seats at a glance.

Slot Booking

Choose a time slot and reserve your seat or resource.

Auto Seat Release

Seats are freed automatically when the booking expires.
